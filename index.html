About Science Guru

Science Guru is a coaching institute in Ahmedabad for students of Classes 10 to 12, with branches in Nikol and New Naroda. We teach Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology through clear, concept-based teaching, supported by weekly tests, complete notes and regular doubt solving.

Our faculty also guide students towards board and competitive examinations. Many of our students go on to secure admission to top colleges.
